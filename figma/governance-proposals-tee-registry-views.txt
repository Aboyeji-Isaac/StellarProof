Design Notes

The designs were prepared to provide a consistent Light Mode experience across the governance and registry areas, with attention to:

Clear visual hierarchy
Consistent spacing and typography
Readable status and health indicators
Reusable design components
Structured frames and layers
Consistent table, list, and card patterns
Responsive layout considerations
